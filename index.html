FAQ

Questions people ask.

How long does it take?

A one-page site takes 3–7 days. A business site takes 1–2 weeks. Online shops take 3–5 weeks. You get the date in your quote.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa. You pay 50% to start and the rest when you approve the site.

Can I change things after launch?

Yes. You get 30 days of free small changes. After that we charge a small fee for updates.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built for phones first and tested on small screens and slow data.

Do you help with the domain name and hosting?

Yes. We set up both for you and they stay in your name. They cost about KES 2,000–6,000 a year.
